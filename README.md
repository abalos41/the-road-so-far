# 🎸 The Road So Far FM

**A classic rock radio station and a mixtape cassette for Project Zomboid (Build 42)**

The world ended, but the rock didn't! 🤘 Inspired by *Supernatural*, this mod adds a classic rock station and a collectible cassette to your game.

---

## 📻 Radio: 99.6 MHz

Tune any radio (house, portable or car) to **99.6 MHz**.

24 rock classics play in rotation, **plus the occasional message from a sponsor you won't forget**. 👀

🎤 **Artists:** ACDC, Asia, Black Sabbath, Blue Öyster Cult, Bob Seger, Bon Jovi, Boston, Cream, Creedence Clearwater Revival, Foreigner, Iron Butterfly, Kansas, Metallica, Mötley Crüe, Motörhead, Survivor, The Animals, The Kinks, The Rolling Stones, Three Dog Night, Van Halen.

---

## 📼 Cassette: Dean's Top 13 Zepp Traxx

A rare mixtape with **13 Led Zeppelin classics**, in order, for any cassette player.

🚗 Look for it in car glove compartments.

---

## 🌍 Built for multiplayer

- 🎶 Everyone near a playing radio or cassette hears the same song at the same time.
- 📡 If you arrive late, you get tuning sounds and short rock edits instead of silence, and then you join in.
- 🔉 The music **fades with distance**.
- 🧟 Loud music can attract zombies. You can turn this off in the sandbox options, which also have a music volume setting.

---

## 🎧 Singleplayer bonus track: your own songs on 100.2 MHz

1. 📂 Open the mod folder (copy this path into the Windows Explorer address bar):

   ```
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\108600\YOUR_WORKSHOP_ID\mods\TheRoadSoFarFM\42
   ```

2. 💿 Copy your **MP3 or OGG** files into:

   ```
   media\sound\Radio\UserTracks
   ```

3. ▶️ Go back to the **42** folder, open **tools**, right-click **Load_My_Songs.cmd** and choose **Run as administrator**.

4. 🔁 Restart the game and tune any radio to **100.2 MHz**.

*Works in singleplayer only. If Steam is installed somewhere else, use that Steam folder instead. Workshop updates may remove your songs, so keep a copy of them and run the .cmd again after an update.*

---

✅ No other mods are required, and nothing vanilla is replaced.
